## Rationale

Back-to-back P-shaped days are rare, so the odds of a third elongated P were low. The thesis was a rotational day: SPY would make an all-time high first, pull all the way back to the previous day's POC, then rotate higher to close up on the day — with ES and NQ moving in sync. The plan was to long the retest of that POC and ride the rotation back up.

---

## Execution

The thesis played out almost exactly as expected. SPY made the early high, pulled back to the previous day's POC, and the entry near the C period lows was well-timed. However, a Trump tweet about Iran hit mid-session and completely changed the market's tempo. Price barcoded for the rest of the day — sideways, grinding action with no directional follow-through. The early morning highs were retested but faded, and the anticipated breakout to the other end never materialized. Theta steadily eroded the call to nothing.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Summer trading conditions were underweighted in the plan. Big money is on vacation, which means less conviction on moves and a much higher chance of churn and barcode action even when the setup is technically correct. Next time, adjust expectations for summer — smaller size, tighter profit targets, or skip the trade entirely if the setup requires a big sustained move.
