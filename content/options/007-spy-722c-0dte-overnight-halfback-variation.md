## Rationale

This was a variation of the SSS-ranked overnight halfback pullback trade — not a clean textbook setup. The key difference was we weren't gapping up; price was sitting right at the edge of yesterday's range in ES and well within range in NQ. In A period, both markets came down to test the overnight halfback but neither got follow-through to the downside. The read was: if we can't go lower from here, value will begin overlapping and we likely grind higher toward the other end of yesterday's range.

---

## Execution

After the halfback test held, both markets began one-time framing higher — the thesis was developing. Then D period failed to continue the one-time frame sequence. There was some bearish momentum but nothing that warranted a panic exit — a little give-back felt within normal range. Then news broke about Iranian attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz. By the time attention returned to the screen, the position had gone from working to worthless.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Two flags were already present before entry: it was a Monday (a day with a history of lower conviction), and the setup was a variation of the playbook rather than the exact SSS-ranked trigger. Those reservations were noted but overridden. The geopolitical news event was unpredictable, but trading a non-standard setup with less margin for error on a low-conviction day stacked the risk. Stay ready so you don't have to get ready.
