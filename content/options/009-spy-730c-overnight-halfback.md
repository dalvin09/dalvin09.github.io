## Rationale

Gap up on positive war ceasefire news. NQ and ES were initially divergent — NQ opened below its overnight halfback level while ES opened above. The thesis was to wait for NQ to recapture its level, because if both indices could hold above their respective levels the next destination was a new all-time high. Once both markets were trading above the levels with breakaway value, the long was confirmed.

---

## Execution

The setup was textbook at entry. There were notable nuances throughout the trade: VIX was spiking all day (unusual for longs), the overnight range was massive, and the market was trading in real time against a live Iran ceasefire proposal — uncertainty was high. Given the backdrop, I accepted I'd either get chopped to bits or catch near the lows of the day. It was the latter. Markets were choppy and mechanical — felt almost purely algo and daytrader driven. Once price broke the overnight high and tagged a new all-time high, tempo died immediately and the market began building three-wide balance. I closed there.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Clean read on the close — recognizing that three-wide balance after a new ATH meant the move was done was the right call. The willingness to hold through the chop given the macro backdrop showed patience and process.
