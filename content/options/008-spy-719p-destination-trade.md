## Rationale

This was the SS-ranked destination trade. Both ES and NQ were holding beneath the overnight high, representing a confirmed failure at that extreme. The thesis was that as long as that failure held, value would build unchanged on the day and the market would bracket — with the other end of range as the destination. The put was the vehicle to fade the overnight high.

---

## Execution

The failure at the overnight high appeared to be holding, and the setup was developing as expected. Heading into the 9am news, the level was taken out entirely. Value did not overlap — it remained breakaway higher on the day. The core condition of the destination trade, the sustained failure at the overnight extreme, was invalidated by the news-driven move.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

The setup read was correct and the pattern was real — the failure was there. The gap was that 9am news was pending and the overnight high was being tested right into that catalyst window. The destination trade depends on range structure holding; news events are capable of overriding that structure entirely. Waiting for the news to print before committing to the fade would have kept this trade off the board. Being on the wrong side of developing value was the other tell — value was already building higher, which conflicted with the bearish destination thesis.
