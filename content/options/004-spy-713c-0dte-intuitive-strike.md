## Rationale

Market opened near all-time highs and made a straight-shot move lower. ES and NQ were moving in sync relative to key market profile levels, and both markets initially left poor lows inside the prior day's value area. Value was still building in a way that could breakaway or overlap to the upside — ES felt like it wanted higher, but NQ was holding it back. Once NQ breached the prior day's value area and left another poor low, ES was already three periods wide. The squeeze was on, with the thesis being a move to the other end of range around the overnight high.

---

## Execution

After the ES three-period squeeze setup confirmed and the NQ poor low formed, the long call was entered at $1.34 on the 713 strike. The rally recaptured the opening print on the day, which served as the exit target given the herky-jerky price action and subtle divergences between ES and NQ throughout the session. Closed the full position at $2.08 into that strength rather than pushing for the overnight high.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Ideally the plan was to scale out 70% and leave a runner toward the overnight high, but with a limited account only 1 contract was available — full close was the only risk-sound option. As the account grows, proper scaling will allow for a more complete expression of the thesis. The divergence between ES and NQ was a real signal worth respecting; closing into the opening print recapture rather than the overnight high was the right read given that context.
