## Rationale

Based on yesterday's action, the thesis was a P-shaped day — a profile structure that suggests value building higher and a move to new highs. PPI came in scorching hot pre-market, which introduced doubt, but the original read on the market held. If price could get breakaway value and escape the prior profile, the path to a new all-time high was open.

---

## Execution

A period slipped the initial reference area in both ES and NQ, sending price into a deeper test of the previous session's value area. NQ was already inside value and showing slight divergence. There was also a poor low left on yesterday's profile, leaving the door open for a deeper flush — but the premium was already paid and the thesis was left to cook. Around noon, SPY made its run to a new all-time high and the full position was closed into that strength at $191.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

At $65 per contract, the position represented roughly 13% of the account — ideally, 13% of the account should cover two contracts so one can be closed for a gain and a runner left on. However, doubling the size would have pushed risk above 20% of total account, which wasn't acceptable. This is a position-sizing constraint tied to account size. As the account grows, this setup will scale properly with two contracts and a defined exit plan for each leg.
