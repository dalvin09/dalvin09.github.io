## Rationale

Starting the night before, the read was that today could be a down day. The profiles from the prior Friday and Monday looked unhealthy, and CPI was coming in the morning — if hot enough, that was the catalyst needed to repair poor structure below. On the open, the ES never got above the overnight halfback, which triggers the SSS Rank overnight halfback trade. The ES and NQ began moving lower, but the NQ wasn't falling the way it should have been — a clear divergence. Both markets then failed the overnight low and started reversing higher. Given the poor low left on yesterday's profile, that failure dramatically shifted the odds toward overlapping value and possibly a new all-time high. A brief reversal to calls was taken to respect what the market was showing.

---

## Execution

**Leg 1 — 735P:** Bought @ $1.00, sold @ $0.87. Early open volatility chopped the position out. (-$13)

**Leg 2 — 738C (reversal):** Bought @ $0.81 after ES/NQ failed the overnight low and structure shifted bullish. Sold @ $0.68 as the thesis broke down quickly. (-$13)

**Leg 3 — 735P (final reversal):** The NQ began pushing higher and was clearly dragging the ES with it. When the NQ recaptured the overnight halfback and broke into yesterday's range, the tempo of the tape changed dramatically — enough to jump out of the chair. Reversed back to puts. First mind had said this was a down day since the night before. Paid the premium knowing the outcome was either getting chopped again or getting paid.

The NQ and ES eventually broke down. Held through the move, then saw a test of a TPO level with no acceptance. Given the herky-jerky nature of the session, the read was that this area could be forming the lower part of a B-day profile — so the position was closed at $2.25 for a +$117 gain on the leg. That level turned out to be the low of the day.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Closing at the TPO level was a logical decision — and the read on B-day structure forming was correct. But the deeper error was walking away entirely instead of staying present and active. The market had been read correctly from the night before: the bias, the catalyst, the structure. At that point, conviction should have been high enough to stay in and manage rather than exit and disengage. The poor low that formed later on both the NQ and ES, followed by the subsequent rally, was a move that could have been caught. This is a recurring pattern — closing too early when the trade has been working instead of continuing to work the position while the market is giving direction.

**Note to self:** When you've had a bead on the market this well, don't just close — stay in the room.
