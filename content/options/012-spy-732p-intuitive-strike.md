## Execution

The trade did not play out. Price was in range and value, which stacked the odds toward chop rather than a clean directional move. ---

## Mistake / Lesson

Being in range and value with no breakaway structure all week was a meaningful conflict with the setup. The intuitive strike performs best when the tape has directional character — in a bracketed, choppy environment, even a correct read gets swallowed by noise. The context filter needs to be applied before the instinct is acted on, not after.
