## Rationale

SSS-ranked purple line level with price interacting at the zone. The setup was there from the start — the level was clean and the rank was as high as it gets. The thesis was straightforward: price tests the purple line, you're in.

---

## Execution

Price came into the level and interacted with it, but instead of executing immediately I held off — I wanted to see the response first. Shortly after, positive war news dropped (ceasefire headlines) and the initial move was explosive. By the time I entered, the bulk of the move was already done. Only managed to snag a few pips out of what should have been a much larger winner.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

When the setup is SSS-ranked, waiting for confirmation is the mistake. The whole point of the purple line at that rank is that the level earns your trust — you don't need to see the response before acting. Hesitating cost the full move. Next time price interacts with an SSS purple line, execute at the level. Period.
