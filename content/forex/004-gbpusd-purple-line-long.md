## Rationale

We recaptured the purple line level and the opening print. The dollar had taken a step lower, adding fuel to the upside in cable. The thesis was that price could pull back a touch but would ultimately continue the uptrend from the prior week — the level gave a clean reference to trade from and context supported the long.

---

## Execution

The level didn't hold. Price chopped around the zone before briefly getting back above, then as London open approached the market firmly slipped through the level. Stopped out clean.

---

## Mistake / Lesson

Even the purple line is no match for a bank holiday in the country of the respective currency. The UK bank holiday threw a wrench in what was otherwise a grounded thesis — thin participation and abnormal conditions distorted the reaction at the level. Going forward, if the currency's home country is observing a bank holiday, wait. Let them return to normal market conditions before trusting the reaction at any level, purple line or otherwise.
