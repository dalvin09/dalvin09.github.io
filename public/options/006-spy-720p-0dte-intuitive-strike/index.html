Rationale#

After closing the earlier long, the intuition said to reverse. The market had gapped up higher with breakaway value but failed to one-time-frame in C period to leave the initial balance higher — that lack of follow-through suggested the market might chop or grind lower into yesterday’s weak structure below. The reversal into the 720 put was the thesis being tested with small size given the 50/50 nature of the read.

Execution#

The market did grind lower initially, validating the directional feel. However when Trump came on the news, the market couldn’t even push lower to recapture the opening print — the expected catalyst produced no downside follow-through. Volatility had come out of the market. That was the signal the thesis was wrong and the position was closed at $0.29.

Mistake / Lesson#

Not every intuitive strike works out — that’s part of the trade. The key was recognizing the thesis was wrong in real time rather than holding through hope. Sizing down because the read was 50/50 was the right call; this is how small losses stay small. The intuition fired, the trade was taken, and when the market spoke clearly, it was closed. That’s the process working correctly even on a losing trade.

Review#

What went well: Recognized the thesis was wrong as volatility left the market. Sized down appropriately for a 50/50 read. Didn’t hold the intuition back and didn’t hold the loss longer than needed.
What to improve: These trades don’t always work — the discipline is in testing with small size and accepting the result cleanly.
Grade: B
