Concept#

The Intuitive Strike is an original trade. It does not belong to any textbook, methodology, or framework. It exists because enough screen time has been accumulated that the subconscious mind begins to recognize market patterns before the analytical mind can catch up and name them. A sudden feeling takes hold. The trade is entered. Only then does the analytical side emerge to validate — never before.

This is not a trade to be recreated. It is documented here as an honest record of how certain trades are taken, and why they work. It is original work — almost a form of art.

Who This Trade Is For#

This trade is only for the trader who has logged an extreme amount of screen time — enough that pattern recognition has moved from conscious analysis to subconscious reflex. Three types of people encounter the little voice:

  • Normal people — ignore it
  • Crazy people — have a conversation with it
  • This trade — stakes capital on it without hesitation

If you need percentages, fixed risk/reward ratios, defined profit targets, or a checklist before entry — this trade will not work for you. Mental friction kills it dead. Hesitation is fatal. Faith in your own skill, writ large, is the only entry requirement.

I cannot in good faith recommend anyone attempt to recreate this trade.

Criteria#

There are no fixed criteria. That is the point.

The trade begins with a feeling — not a signal. A sudden, internal certainty that the market is about to do something specific. The position is entered before that feeling can be second-guessed. The analytical process begins only after the position is on.

What typically follows the entry — not before it — may include:

  • Recognizing market profile structure that supports the move
  • Noting confluence between correlated instruments (e.g. ES vs NQ divergence/convergence)
  • Identifying a level that price was already moving toward

These are confirmations, not triggers. The trigger is the feeling itself.

Entry#

  • Trigger: Internal — a subconscious recognition of pattern that presents as a feeling or sudden certainty
  • Entry: Immediate. No hesitation. No confirmation wait.
  • Stop: Contextual — determined after entry based on what the analytical mind identifies as the invalidation point
  • Target: Contextual — the nearest significant structural level the market is being pulled toward
  • Sizing: Appropriate to account size and risk tolerance — this trade does not override risk management, it simply bypasses analytical entry gates

Weaknesses & When It Fails#

  • Hesitation — any delay between the feeling and the entry degrades the trade. The window is narrow.
  • Mental friction — second-guessing, demanding confirmation, running through a checklist. This kills it.
  • Fatigue or distraction — the subconscious needs a clean signal environment. A noisy mental state produces noise, not insight.
  • Trying to recreate it analytically — it cannot be reverse-engineered. Attempting to build a rule set from past examples will produce a broken imitation.

Notes#

This trade is documented not as instruction but as record. It is the product of accumulated screen time that cannot be transferred or taught in any conventional sense. The edge is real. The process is real. But the prerequisite — the depth of subconscious market fluency required — is years in the making and entirely personal.

If this trade resonates with you because you have experienced something similar in your own trading, you already know what it is. No further explanation is necessary.
