Rationale#

SSS ranked profile switch in the pound. The market was getting extended and the thesis was simple: if price holds the previous profile’s close and pushes higher, that’s a textbook profile switch entry. The setup quality was high and aligned with the playbook, making it a valid reason to engage.

Execution#

Entry was taken at 1.35188 on 20 lots. After getting in, the position was doubled to press the advantage — but scaling up breached the account’s max drawdown limit, triggering an auto-liquidation. The trade closed at 1.35177 for a -$300 loss. The original thesis wasn’t disproven by price — the account rules ended the trade.

Mistake / Lesson#

Doubling the position size without accounting for proximity to the max drawdown threshold was the critical error. The setup was SSS, but position sizing must always respect account risk limits first. More size is only an edge if the account survives to benefit from it. There may also be a deeper gap in conviction or understanding of how forex markets move — worth revisiting before the next funded attempt.
