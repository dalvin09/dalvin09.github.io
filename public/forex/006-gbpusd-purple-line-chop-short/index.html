Rationale#

SSS-ranked purple line setup in GBP/USD. The level warranted the short — rank was as high as it gets and the setup was there. Came in short looking for price to follow through off the zone.

Execution#

Got chopped to bits. Price didn’t give a clean follow-through and kept cutting back and forth at the level. Couldn’t squeeze the nuance out of this setup this time around. Ended up on the wrong side of the chop and took the -$700 hit.

Mistake / Lesson#

This setup is crazy nuanced. Not every SSS-ranked purple line resolves cleanly — the market doesn’t always give you the easy version. The lesson here isn’t to stop taking it, it’s to accept that even the highest-ranked setups will have losing weeks.
